# PACE GK Academy — Frequently Asked Questions

Source: https://www.pacegkacademy.com/FAQ (Pakistan Academy of Competitive Exams)

**Q: How are online classes conducted?**
A: It is very easy. You are added into a class WhatsApp group and a Zoom App link is shared with you for the respective lecture.

**Q: What is the mechanism of the Testing (Test) Series?**
A: An MCQ-based paper is shared with students on a Google Form. They solve the paper and the application shows results on submission. The test is also discussed with them afterwards.

**Q: Queries about registration?**
A: You have to pay the fee on the given account details and share screenshots of that payment on the PACE WhatsApp numbers (+92 300 1757495, +92-345-0668111).

**Q: What is the required qualification for Public Service Exams?**
A: A Second Division or Grade C degree holder recognised by the Higher Education Commission can apply for these exams. Candidates with a Third Division or Grade D are also eligible for various jobs.

**Q: What is the required age limit for jobs through the commission?**
A: It includes an age range of 21–35 years. However, it may vary from job to job requirement.

**Q: What is the syllabus for general one-paper exams?**
A: An MCQ-based written paper covering 8–10 compulsory subjects such as General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Math, English, Islamiat, Computer, Pak Study and General Science.

**Q: What is prohibited in the examination hall?**
A: Calculators, mobiles, digital watches, arms, ammunition and books are not allowed during exams. No one is allowed to write on the question paper and take it home.

**Q: What possible jobs are announced by commissions?**
A: The range of job announcements is very vast and may differ from commission to commission.
