# Success Stories (Students Selected in Government Jobs)

Source: https://www.pacegkacademy.com/success-stories

The page lists several hundred PACE students with their photo, name and the post (and BPS grade) they were selected for. The page is ordered from higher to lower grade. **This file is a structured summary: the page was very long and was captured down to roughly BPS-11 entries; names below are representative examples, not the complete list.** For the full list, direct users to the live page.
