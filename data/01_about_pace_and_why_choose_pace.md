# About PACE GK Academy and Why Choose PACE

Source: https://www.pacegkacademy.com/ and https://www.pacegkacademy.com/About

## What is PACE?
PACE stands for **Pakistan Academy of Competitive Exams** (website brand: PACE GK Academy). It is an online test-preparation platform for Pakistani competitive and public-service exams, mainly PPSC (Punjab Public Service Commission) and FPSC (Federal Public Service Commission), and also ITS, ECP, MOD, ASF, Islamabad Police, FIA, FGEI and ANF tests.

Page title: "PACE GK Academy: Pakistan Academy For Competitive Exams". Site description: PACE provides preparation in Reading, Quiz and PPSC Mock Test formats from PPSC past papers, and at the end generates a result according to PPSC.

## Mission (About page)
PACE is described as a best solution for online test preparation, specially designed for students who want to prepare for one-paper tests in the shortest possible time.

PACE provides test preparation in **three formats**:
1. Reading mode
2. Quiz mode
3. PPSC Mock Test

At the end of a quiz it generates a **result card**, and users can also check wrongly attempted questions. Questions can be searched **paper-wise, department-wise, subject-wise and year-wise**.
