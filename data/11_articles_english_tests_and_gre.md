## 2. How to Score 320+ in GRE — Tips & Resources
URL: https://www.pacegkacademy.com/How-to-score-320-plus-in-GRE-tips-and-resources
- A 320 means roughly 160 Verbal + 160 Quant. Study time: 8–12 weeks (intensive); plan adjustable to 8–16 weeks.
- **Strategy:** take a full-length diagnostic (official ETS GRE practice); focus on Quant fundamentals and Verbal vocabulary; timed sections; answer elimination and pacing; review mistakes; use spaced repetition (Anki/Magoosh Vocab); simulate test day with at least 4 full-length tests.
- **12-week plan:** Weeks 1–2 diagnostic, math fundamentals, 20–30 new vocab words/day. Weeks 3–6 timed Quant & Verbal practice 3×/week, learn problem types (DS, PS, RC, SC), one full-length test at week 6. Weeks 7–12 polish weak areas, one simulated test per week, time management, last 2 weeks taper and rest.
- **Resources:** Official GRE/ETS POWERPREP, Magoosh GRE, Manhattan Prep, Kaplan GRE, Princeton Review, Anki.
- **Test-day tips:** book a slot when you are sharpest; arrive early; mark and skip hard questions; use the on-screen calculator where allowed; stay hydrated; avoid heavy study the night before.

## 4. PTE vs IELTS — Which Test Should You Take?
URL: https://www.pacegkacademy.com/PTE-vs-IELTS-Which-Test-Should-You-Take

| Feature | PTE Academic | IELTS (Academic & General) |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Fully computer-based; speaking via microphone | Paper or computer; speaking with a human examiner |
| Duration | ~2 hours | ~2 h 45 min (varies) |
| Scoring | 10–90 | Band 0–9 |
| Results | Usually within 48 hours | Paper ~13 days; computer ~3–5 days |
| Acceptance | Many universities and some governments; growing | Most universities, governments, professional bodies |
| Cost | Varies by country | Varies by country |

**Rough score mapping:** PTE 79–90 ≈ IELTS 8.5–9.0; 65–78 ≈ 7.0–8.0; 59–64 ≈ 6.5; 50–58 ≈ 5.5–6.0; 30–49 ≈ 4.0–5.0.
**PTE suits:** fast typists comfortable with microphones, want quick results. **IELTS suits:** prefer a human speaking examiner or need widest acceptance.
**FAQ:** PTE may be accepted for immigration (check official guidance); cost varies by centre; you can take both.
**Test-day checklist:** book early, use official practice tests, simulate timing at least 3 times, manage time, bring ID and arrive early. If unsure whether your target universities accept PTE, prefer IELTS.
